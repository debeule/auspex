Item 1A. Risk Factors
Except as set forth below, there have been no material changes to the risk factors in our Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2023.
The FDA has extended its review of our New Drug Application for acmetinib, which may delay approval and commercial launch.
If the FDA does not approve acmetinib by the extended action date, we may need to raise additional capital sooner than planned, and on terms that dilute our stockholders.
