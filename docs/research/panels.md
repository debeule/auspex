# Research panels

Domain: RES · Code: `services/backtesting/src/auspex_backtesting/ownership/`, `services/backtesting/src/auspex_backtesting/short_interest/`, `services/backtesting/src/auspex_backtesting/catalysts/`, `services/backtesting/src/auspex_backtesting/filings/`, `services/backtesting/src/auspex_backtesting/scores/` · Last reviewed against: develop @ `b7582c3`

## Scope

The point-in-time panels the slow-signal hypotheses read, all kept as snapshots in the `auspex-prices` bucket: SEC ownership (13F holdings, insider transactions, offerings), FINRA short interest, scheduled binary catalysts (PDUFA dates and FDA advisory committees), the Item 1A filing-text change score (H10) and the holdings composite score (H9). The shared contract is stated once; each panel's own rules follow.

Not in this file: the universe whose companies the panels hold ([universe.md](universe.md)); the price snapshots and split histories ([market-data.md](market-data.md)); how a portfolio backtest reads score snapshots ([backtesting.md](backtesting.md#RES-075)); SEC's and the Federal Register's access rules ([connectors.md](../ingestion/connectors.md)).

## Machine-readable authorities

- `config/hypotheses/h9.yaml` — every H9 parameter: the floor (`universe.liquidity_floor`, `universe.no_floor`), the components and their keys (`components.specialist_13f_ownership.thresholds`, `components.net_insider_buying.window_days`, `components.net_insider_buying.excludes`), `score`, `component_variants` and the trade-date rule.
- `config/hypotheses/h10.yaml` — H10's uses and the bounds of its broad universe.
- `config/short_interest/finra_calendar.csv` — FINRA's published settlement and publication dates, month by month.

## Requirements

*Shared panel contract.*

- <a id="RES-026"></a>**RES-026** — Every panel row carries the instant it became known (an acceptance time, a publication time), never a period, settlement, trade or event date in its place, and every read is `as_of` an instant: it returns only rows known before that instant, so a later row or revision never changes an earlier read.
- <a id="RES-027"></a>**RES-027** — Panel stores are append-only or write-once: a stored period, data set, document or snapshot is never rewritten; new rows are added beside it.
- <a id="RES-028"></a>**RES-028** — Missing data is missing with a reason, never zero and never carried forward from an older period.
- <a id="RES-029"></a>**RES-029** — Each fetched research document (a data set, an index, a filing page, an exhibit, a notice, a short-interest period) is fetched once and read from the bucket after that; a rerun fetches only what is not yet stored.
- <a id="RES-030"></a>**RES-030** — Panels hold universe companies only and refuse to build before the universe is built. They are snapshots in `auspex-prices` built by the backtesting service, not ingestion connectors, and never pass through the document pipeline.

*Ownership.*

- <a id="RES-031"></a>**RES-031** — An ownership row is known at its `filing_accepted_at`: for a bulk data set row, 22:00 US Eastern on its filing date (the latest EDGAR accepts), stored in UTC; for a daily Form 4 row, the real acceptance time from the submission header. Every ownership join uses `filing_accepted_at`, never `period_of_report` or `transaction_date`. An `as_of` date is read as 00:00 UTC that day, and a naive datetime is refused.
- <a id="RES-032"></a>**RES-032** — Each 13F data set is downloaded once and stored under SEC's own label (a calendar quarter or a three-month window), keeping the holdings whose issuer maps to a universe company, written once (share counts excepted, which are replaced on every run). An issuer's CUSIP maps to a CIK by its normalized issuer names against every operating company's current and former names (a name shared by several companies maps to none, the most-voted company wins, a tie resolves nothing, a shared six-character issuer prefix settles the rest); unmapped CUSIPs are counted with their value per data set, never dropped. The 13F report as of a date is a filer's latest restatement accepted before it (else its latest original) plus every new-holdings amendment accepted after that base; options and principal-amount rows are left out, and values before 2023 are read in thousands.
- <a id="RES-033"></a>**RES-033** — `SpecialistClassifier`: a 13F filer is a healthcare specialist as of a date when, over its reports for the four calendar quarters before that date, at least `SPECIALIST_HEALTHCARE_SHARE` of its long equity value is in issuers with SIC 2834, 2836, 8731 or 3841 (by the issuer's current SIC), and its latest of those reports totals at least `SPECIALIST_MIN_AUM_USD`. Options and principal amounts are left out of both sides. Only filings accepted before the date count, and the classification is derived from the filings, never from a hand-made list of funds. Both thresholds are read only from the registered `config/hypotheses/h9.yaml` (`components.specialist_13f_ownership.thresholds`), after its hash is verified: an unregistered or edited file, or one without the thresholds, is refused. Holdings of filers that are not specialists do not count.
- <a id="RES-034"></a>**RES-034** — Insider rows are the original Forms 4 and 5 of universe issuers (amendments dropped), non-derivative transactions only, a joint filing attributed once to its lowest owner CIK; a transaction is a 10b5-1 plan trade when the filing, its remarks or a footnote it references says so. `net_insider_buying` counts open-market purchases (code `P`) minus sales (code `S`) accepted in the window H9 registers, over market cap, leaving out 10b5-1 plan trades and purchases flagged `offering_participation` (near a `424B4` or `424B5` of the same issuer, as `components.net_insider_buying.excludes` in `h9.yaml` states, counting only offerings public before the read). Daily Form 4 rows fill a quarter until its data set exists; the data set is then preferred and any disagreement is reported by filing.
- <a id="RES-035"></a>**RES-035** — `specialist_ownership(issuer_cik, as_of)` is the shares specialists hold in each one's latest report accepted before `as_of` over the shares outstanding filed before `as_of`, both restated to `as_of`'s split basis (each report from its own period, the count from its own date). The read is missing with its reason when the basis cannot be vouched for: `no_share_count`, `ticker_history_uncertain` (an old `filing` ticker, a reused or changed symbol, or a month without the member), `split_history_unavailable` (including a history that does not reach `as_of`) or `multiple_share_classes`. An issuer no specialist holds reads zero.
- <a id="RES-036"></a>**RES-036** — The ownership panels report the input times of each read: `specialist_ownership_known_at` is the latest acceptance among the reports and the share count the read used, and `net_insider_buying_known_at` is the acceptance of the latest transaction it counted.

*Short interest.*

- <a id="RES-037"></a>**RES-037** — A short-interest period is known from its FINRA publication date, by the end of that day, never from its settlement date. Settlement dates are the 15th and the month's last settlement day moved back to an NYSE session; publication follows FINRA's rule, and a month listed in `config/short_interest/finra_calendar.csv` takes the published dates instead. Periods published after the fetch's cutoff are left out.
- <a id="RES-038"></a>**RES-038** — A short-interest record holds `settlement_date`, `publication_date`, `reported`, `short_interest_shares`, `average_daily_volume` and `days_to_cover`, the exact ratio of the two counts (`None` at zero volume). A ticker missing from a period FINRA published is stored `reported = false` with no counts: absent, not zero. Periods are appended to a ticker's file and never rewritten, and the coverage report gives the first available settlement date, empty periods and gaps.
- <a id="RES-039"></a>**RES-039** — The derived short-interest share of shares outstanding and the change since the previous period are computed on one split basis: short interest from its settlement date and the count from its own date are restated to the date read, and the previous period to the current settlement date, through the ownership panels' split factors. A figure that cannot be put on one basis is missing with its reason (`not_reported`, `no_share_count`, `ticker_history_uncertain`, `split_history_unavailable`).
- <a id="RES-040"></a>**RES-040** — Short interest is fetched from `SHORT_INTEREST_HISTORY_START` (its value lives in `.env.example`) with a FINRA API credential; a client without a credential refuses to start.

*Catalysts.*

- <a id="RES-041"></a>**RES-041** — PDUFA dates come from phrase rules over Exhibit 99.1 of universe companies' 8-Ks with Item 7.01 or 8.01, and are known at the 8-K's acceptance time. FDA advisory committee meetings come from Federal Register meeting notices and are known at 06:00 US Eastern on the publication date. A notice whose sponsor matches no universe company, or several, is stored without a CIK and counted; cancellation and postponement notices are counted, not applied. No LLM is involved.
- <a id="RES-042"></a>**RES-042** — A catalyst date keeps the precision it was written at: `day`, `month`, `quarter` or `half`. A bare year gives no row.
- <a id="RES-043"></a>**RES-043** — A revision is a new row, never an edit: rows are appended by source and quarter of disclosure, and `as_of` returns the latest row per catalyst (rows of one company and type sharing an alias; rows naming no product revise only each other). Every document read is kept under `catalyst-documents/` in `auspex-prices`, except a quarter's index pages while that quarter is still in progress.
- <a id="RES-044"></a>**RES-044** — `as_of` and `binaries_between` take timezone-aware times and refuse naive ones. `binaries_between` returns the catalysts known at the trade date whose period overlaps the window, including past ones that `as_of` drops.

*Filing text (H10).*

- <a id="RES-045"></a>**RES-045** — EDGAR's quarterly `master.idx` rows for 10-K, 10-K405, 10-KT and 10-Q filings and their amendments are stored once per finished quarter (a quarter in progress is read again on the next run), and universe membership is applied when the rows are used, so one index serves every universe.
- <a id="RES-046"></a>**RES-046** — Original filings are cut and amendments are only counted. Item 1A is cut by heading rules (a heading only at the start of a line, the longest span to the next end heading) and kept with its acceptance time, form and period; a filing whose section cannot be cut is kept as `section_missing` with its reason. A short section saying there are no material changes is `no_change_statement` and scores zero change; a section saying the item is not required is `section_missing` with `not_provided`, so it is missing, not unchanged.
- <a id="RES-047"></a>**RES-047** — A filing's comparison is the same form family's filing from the same CIK whose period of report is a year earlier within the period tolerance (`_PERIOD_TOLERANCE_DAYS` in `services/backtesting/src/auspex_backtesting/filings/score.py`) and which was accepted before it. Change is 1 − the cosine similarity of term frequencies (lowercase letter tokens, no stemming), Jaccard similarity alongside, and the score is known at the later filing's acceptance time.
- <a id="RES-048"></a>**RES-048** — The monthly read takes, for each member of the month's universe snapshot, the latest score accepted before the rebalance session's open within the score lookback (`SCORE_LOOKBACK` in `services/backtesting/src/auspex_backtesting/filings/score.py`), with `score = −change`, so the least changed ranks highest; members without one are left out and counted. Snapshots of one H10 version are kept apart per universe (the universe id as a version suffix), and a month's snapshot is written only once its rebalance day has passed.
- <a id="RES-049"></a>**RES-049** — A refusal from SEC stops the filing-panel run before scoring, with no snapshot written; the next run continues from what was archived. Requests carry the configured `User-Agent`.

*Holdings composite (H9).*

- <a id="RES-050"></a>**RES-050** — Every H9 parameter (components, windows, floor, score rule, variants, trade-date rule) is read from the registered `config/hypotheses/h9.yaml`, and every input is read as of the rebalance session's open. Days to cover comes from FINRA's latest period published before the rebalance day.
- <a id="RES-051"></a>**RES-051** — H9's floor reads market cap and median dollar volume from the month's universe snapshot, and an unknown value counts as under it. The `no_floor` cells use the universe's own floors and are written under their own score version, so floored and unfloored snapshots never mix.
- <a id="RES-052"></a>**RES-052** — Each component follows its missing rule (specialist ownership missing with its share-basis reason, net insider buying zero with no activity and missing without a market cap, days to cover missing when the ticker is absent from the latest period), with no carry-forward. Each component's percentile rank (best 1.0, worst 1/n, ties broken by CIK) is taken among the names scored that month; a name needs at least two components, and its score is the mean of its ranks. `known_at_max` is the latest of its inputs' times and falls before the rebalance open.
- <a id="RES-053"></a>**RES-053** — The component variant is chosen from coverage dates only: two components when stored short interest starts after the in-sample start (or there is none), three otherwise, recorded in each row's `variant`; never chosen by looking at returns.
- <a id="RES-054"></a>**RES-054** — An H9 snapshot is written once per (score version, floor, rebalance date), and every row passes `ScoreSnapshot.validate()`. The coverage report gives the variant, per month the members, under the floor, scored and left out, and per year the correlation of each pair of component ranks.

## Known traps

- **No panel is kept current by the stack.** The ownership, short-interest, catalyst, filing and score panels are built by scripts run once on the stack machine (`services/backtesting/README.md`, Scripts); no DAG or endpoint refreshes them. Sources: `## 2026-10-09 — SEC ownership datasets — FLAG — panels are refreshed by a script, not a DAG` and `## 2026-10-09 — Catalyst date panel — FLAG — not kept current by the stack` in `DECISIONS.md`. The refresh is proposed in `specs/slow-signal-data-refresh.md`; until it merges, do not describe it as current.
- **Test fixtures follow documented layouts, not real files.** The SEC data-set readmes leave the header row and some field values open, and cloud sessions cannot download the files; the first run on the stack machine checks them (`## 2026-10-09 — SEC ownership datasets — VERIFIED — data set sources, layouts and start dates`).
- **Two catalyst sources are planned.** `specs/catalyst-date-panel.md` (phrase rules, merged: RES-041 to RES-044) and `specs/catalyst-calendar.md` (LLM-based, not built). Only the panel exists; how the two relate is decided when the calendar is scoped.

## Rationale

- **Insider-copying event trades are not built.** The research found the reaction to an insider purchase ends within two to three sessions, too fast for this trader, so insider data enters only as a panel component (`specs/sec-ownership-datasets.md`, Out of scope).
- **H10 has two uses.** Inside biotech it is an exclusion filter on H9; on broad US small and mid caps it is a standalone test, which is why the filing panel serves two universes (`specs/filing-text-change-score.md`).
- **Sections, not documents, are archived.** The broad universe is tens of thousands of filings over the window, so the panel keeps the cut Item 1A text, not the full filings (`specs/filing-text-change-score.md`, Constraints).
