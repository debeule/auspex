# Market simulation

Domain: RES · Code: `services/backtesting/src/auspex_backtesting/market_sim/` · Last reviewed against: develop @ `b7582c3`

## Scope

How a backtest and the strategy runtime turn a decision into a trade: NYSE sessions, fills at the open and close, stop fills, the cost of a trade (commission, Belgian tax on stock exchange transactions, spread, FX fee, borrow fee), the volume cap, EUR/USD conversion, position sizing and the check that a universe's snapshots cover the dates a run needs.

Cost values are environment variables named in `.env.example` (`IBKR_*`, `SPREAD_BPS`, `MIN_HALF_SPREAD_BPS`, `TOB_RATE`, `FX_FEE_RATE`, `BORROW_FEE_ANNUAL_PCT`, `MAX_ADV_FRACTION`); this file names them and never copies their defaults.

Not in this file: when a backtest enters and exits ([backtesting.md](backtesting.md)); the price snapshots the classes read ([market-data.md](market-data.md)); risk limits and position sizing in the strategy runtime ([risk.md](../strategy/risk.md)).

## Requirements

- <a id="RES-055"></a>**RES-055** — `MarketCalendar` gives NYSE sessions, holidays and early closes, with session opens and closes in UTC.
- <a id="RES-056"></a>**RES-056** — `FillModel` enters at the open of the date it is given and exits at the close of the exit session; it never shifts an entry date (the caller decides the entry session), and a missing price raises instead of borrowing a neighbouring bar.
- <a id="RES-057"></a>**RES-057** — A stop the price gaps through fills at the open; an ordinary intraday cross fills at the stop; on a day the caller flags as a binary event a touched stop fills at the day's low for a long and the day's high for a short; a stop the range stays clear of does not trigger, and a missing bar raises.
- <a id="RES-058"></a>**RES-058** — `CostModel` costs each leg as commission on IBKR's fixed or tiered pricing (`IBKR_PRICING`), with tiered pass-through exchange and clearing fees per share, plus the Belgian tax on stock exchange transactions (`TOB_RATE`), the half-spread, an FX conversion fee per leg (`FX_FEE_RATE`), and for a short a flat annual borrow fee (`BORROW_FEE_ANNUAL_PCT`). Its minimum ticket is the minimum commission divided by IBKR's 1% commission cap; an order below it is skipped by the caller, not costed above the cap. All values come from the environment through `CostParameters.from_env`.
- <a id="RES-059"></a>**RES-059** — With a `SpreadEstimator`, the half-spread is the Abdi–Ranaldo estimate over sessions strictly before the entry date, skipping bars with missing or non-positive prices; a negative estimate reads as zero, and the result is floored at `MIN_HALF_SPREAD_BPS`. Fewer than two usable prior sessions raises. Without an estimator the flat `SPREAD_BPS` applies and the entry date is not needed.
- <a id="RES-060"></a>**RES-060** — The volume cap limits a position to `MAX_ADV_FRACTION` of the average daily volume over sessions strictly before entry; a cap below one share, or a stock that did not trade, rejects the position, and no volume history raises.
- <a id="RES-061"></a>**RES-061** — `fx_rate` is EUR per USD: `CurrencyConverter.eur_per_usd` is `1 / EURUSD=X close`, and a missing FX bar raises.
- <a id="RES-062"></a>**RES-062** — The FX rate used for an order is a close known before that order.
- <a id="RES-063"></a>**RES-063** — `PositionSizer` floors to whole shares, keeps the affordable shares when they are below the volume cap, and raises when capital buys no share.
- <a id="RES-064"></a>**RES-064** — `TradableUniverse` validates that each ticker's snapshot covers the dates a run needs, over a member's membership span only, and names the ticker and dates when it does not.
- <a id="RES-065"></a>**RES-065** — The strategy service imports these classes from the backtesting package as a path dependency and never copies them, so a backtest and the runtime cost and fill a trade the same way.

## Rationale

- **This file owns the cost semantics.** The docstring of `CostParameters` in `services/backtesting/src/auspex_backtesting/market_sim/costs.py` still says its defaults match the cost estimates in `docs/strategy-research.md`; that file is non-normative rationale, and the rules are RES-058 to RES-060. Correcting the docstring is a follow-up under `## 2026-10-17 — Living docs — CHOICE — defects found while scoping`.
- **The minimum ticket replaces the commission cap.** IBKR caps commission at 1% of trade value; rather than model the cap, an order small enough to reach it is skipped and counted, so a small account's costs are never understated.
