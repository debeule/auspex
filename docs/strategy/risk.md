# Strategy risk

Domain: STR · Code: `services/strategy/src/auspex_strategy/risk/`, `services/strategy/src/auspex_strategy/runtime/risk.py` · Last reviewed against: develop @ `b7582c3`

## Scope

The risk layer between a strategy's intents and its book: the risk limits and their startup check, Kelly sizing, position, theme and exposure limits, the binary-event guard, the short guard, the daily-loss, turnover and drawdown limits, the kill switch, the `RiskDecision` the runtime receives, and capital allocation across promoted strategies.

The library is implemented and tested, but the streaming runtime does not use it yet: it reviews intents with a pass-through stub and enforces only the kill switch (STR-053). Until that is wired, every requirement here except STR-051 holds for the library and its tests, not for paper trading.

Not in this file: the runtime that sends intents to risk ([runtime.md](runtime.md), STR-031); the kill-switch command line ([runtime.md](runtime.md), STR-040); traces of risk decisions ([decision-trace.md](decision-trace.md)); the protocol's sizing and instrument ceilings themselves ([EVA-005](../research/evaluation-protocol.md#EVA-005) and the protocol file); the catalyst and short-interest panels ([panels.md](../research/panels.md)); the cross-sectional portfolio sizer, which portfolio strategies use instead of this layer ([backtesting.md](../research/backtesting.md)).

## Machine-readable authorities

- `config/hypotheses/protocol.yaml` — `sizing` (the ceilings the `.env` limits may tighten but not exceed, and the floor on trades before Kelly) and `instruments` (whether shorts are enabled). Locked by hash ([EVA-001](../research/evaluation-protocol.md#EVA-001)); this file names the keys and never copies a value.
- `config/themes.yaml` — `tickers`, a map from ticker to correlated theme.
- `.env.example`, block "Risk limits" — one variable per limit, with its unit. The values live there and in `.env`.

## Requirements

*Limits.*
- <a id="STR-041"></a>**STR-041** — Every risk limit is an `.env` variable named after its `RiskLimits` field. Loading the limits verifies the protocol file against its registered hash, and fails, naming the variable, when a limit is missing or unparseable or is looser than the protocol's `sizing` ceiling: `KELLY_FRACTION`, `MAX_POSITION_PCT`, `FALLBACK_POSITION_PCT` and `MAX_POSITIONS_PER_THEME` may not exceed theirs, and `MIN_TRADES_FOR_KELLY` may not go below `sizing.min_trades_for_kelly`.
- <a id="STR-042"></a>**STR-042** — `*_PCT` limits on capital are fractions of capital. `MAX_BORROW_FEE_ANNUAL_PCT`, `MAX_SHORT_INTEREST_PCT` and `SHORT_HARD_STOP_PCT` are percentages, the units of their data sources and of `StopLossPct`. `DAILY_LOSS_LIMIT_EUR` is negative.

*Review.*
- <a id="STR-043"></a>**STR-043** — `RiskManager.evaluate` runs its checks in this order: drawdown; kill switch; sizing; position limits (strategy position count, theme, per-name cap across all strategies, gross exposure, net exposure); binary-event guard; short guard (shorts only); daily loss and turnover. The first failing check rejects the intent, and the decision lists every check that ran. Later checks see the share count the per-name cap left.
- <a id="STR-044"></a>**STR-044** — Kelly sizing estimates the win probability `p` and the win/loss ratio `b` from the strategy's closed trades, shrinks only `p` toward one half by `KELLY_SHRINKAGE_FACTOR`, multiplies full Kelly by `KELLY_FRACTION` and caps the result at `MAX_POSITION_PCT` of capital. Below `MIN_TRADES_FOR_KELLY` closed trades the position is `FALLBACK_POSITION_PCT` of capital. An intent with no Kelly edge is rejected as `POSITION_TOO_SMALL`.
- <a id="STR-045"></a>**STR-045** — An intent is rejected when it would exceed `MAX_POSITIONS_PER_STRATEGY`, `MAX_GROSS_EXPOSURE` or `MAX_NET_EXPOSURE` (in either direction, a short counting in its own); the per-name cap lowers its share count when the name is already held. A held position without a price rejects the intent rather than counting as zero.
- <a id="STR-046"></a>**STR-046** — At most `MAX_POSITIONS_PER_THEME` positions are open per theme across all strategies, with themes read from `config/themes.yaml`. A ticker the map does not list is rejected as `THEME_UNMAPPED`, never treated as uncorrelated.
- <a id="STR-047"></a>**STR-047** — No position opens within `CATALYST_GUARD_DAYS` calendar days before a known binary event, read point in time from the catalyst panel ([RES-041](../research/panels.md#RES-041) to [RES-044](../research/panels.md#RES-044)). A ticker the source cannot answer for (no CIK) is rejected as `CATALYST_UNKNOWN`. A date written imprecisely counts when any part of its period falls in the window. An open position exits at the last session before the period starts, or the next session when that has passed. A long may enter or hold through the catalyst types its strategy declares in `trades_catalyst_types`, and each such entry or hold is recorded; a short never may. Readouts have no source and are not guarded.
- <a id="STR-048"></a>**STR-048** — While the protocol's `instruments` disable shorts ([EVA-005](../research/evaluation-protocol.md#EVA-005)), every short intent is rejected as `SHORTS_DISABLED` before the short guard's own checks.
- <a id="STR-049"></a>**STR-049** — When shorts are enabled, a short is rejected above `MAX_BORROW_FEE_ANNUAL_PCT`, `MAX_SHORT_INTEREST_PCT` (of shares outstanding) or `MAX_DAYS_TO_COVER`. Short interest is the period published by the session before the decision ([RES-037](../research/panels.md#RES-037)); with no data for the ticker the crowding checks are skipped with a warning. The borrow fee is the one flat `BORROW_FEE_ANNUAL_PCT` of the cost model. An open short exits at `SHORT_HARD_STOP_PCT` against its entry or at `MAX_LOSS_PER_SHORT_EUR`.
- <a id="STR-050"></a>**STR-050** — When today's realised plus unrealised P&L is below `DAILY_LOSS_LIMIT_EUR`, new intents are blocked for the rest of the day, and the block lifts the next day. Today's buys plus sells may not exceed `DAILY_TURNOVER_CEILING_PCT` of capital. A drawdown past `MAX_DRAWDOWN_PCT` from the peak book value turns the global kill switch on; a drawdown at the limit does not.
- <a id="STR-051"></a>**STR-051** — The kill switch is global or per strategy and blocks new intents only: open positions keep their exits. Each trigger and reset is appended to the kill-switch event log, and a per-strategy switch accepts only a strategy name.
- <a id="STR-052"></a>**STR-052** — The runtime's risk review returns one `RiskDecision` per intent, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED` or `PAUSED`, with every check that ran. A stop by the drawdown, kill-switch or daily-loss check is `PAUSED`; any other failure is `REJECTED`. `PortfolioRiskReview` counts an event's earlier approvals toward the position limits and turnover of its later intents.

*Wiring and allocation.*
- <a id="STR-053"></a>**STR-053** — The streaming runtime reviews every intent through `RiskManager` and executes the exit intents it produces (catalyst exits, short stops).
- <a id="STR-054"></a>**STR-054** — Capital across concurrent promoted strategies follows an equal risk budget per strategy, with the combined allocation of a highly correlated pair capped below two budgets.

## Known traps

- `config/themes.yaml` maps only the watched tickers, so any event strategy trading another universe ticker is rejected as `THEME_UNMAPPED` (STR-046). Portfolio strategies size with the protocol sizer, not this layer.
- The daily-loss state lives in memory in `services/strategy/src/auspex_strategy/risk/flow.py`: a restart forgets today's P&L and lifts a block early (STR-021 in [runtime.md](runtime.md)).
- The correlation threshold and pair cap of STR-054 are named in its decision as `.env` parameters, but no such variable exists in `.env.example`; whoever implements the allocator adds them there.

## Rationale

- Drawdown runs before the kill switch so a breach turns the global switch on even when the intent in hand would fail a later check (`## 2026-10-11 — Portfolio and risk — CHOICE — catalyst and short-interest sources, units and check order`, item 8).
- Few, correlated, fat-tailed bets make full Kelly ruinous, so `.env` may only tighten the protocol's ceilings (`## 2026-10-07 — Pre-registration — CHOICE — sizing ceilings: quarter Kelly, 5% per name, one position per theme, no hold through binary events`).
